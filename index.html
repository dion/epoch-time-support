What it does

  • Converts Unix timestamps to readable dates the moment you paste them, and dates back to timestamps. It detects seconds, milliseconds and ISO 8601 automatically.
  • Live epoch clock, LOCAL and UTC output, relative time, and your favourite time zones side by side.
  • Paste from anywhere with one tap, and tap to copy or share any result.
  • New in 1.1: a Home Screen widget, batch conversion from the share sheet, and Siri & Shortcuts support.
  • No accounts and no tracking of what you convert. Everything stays on your device.
